同来源合并，不计为独立证据；保留条件差异与矛盾。没有实跑的方法标“来源核查，未实跑”，不把宣传写成效果保证。

Merge repeated references to the same source; they are not independent evidence. Preserve conditional differences and disagreements. Mark methods not run as “source reviewed, not executed”; do not turn promotional claims into guarantees.
