## 工具与方法｜Tools and Methods

问题、输入、前提、步骤、输出、失败信号。无方法的宣传内容只分类。
Question, inputs, prerequisites, steps, outputs, and failure signals. Classify promotional content without forcing it into a method.

## 限制与排除｜Limits and Exclusions

未听校、缺页、缺评论、不可达来源及声音专属未确认内容。保留失败，不从分母删除。
Unreviewed audio, missing pages/comments, unavailable sources, and unverified audio-only claims. Keep failures visible rather than removing them from the denominator.
